/frontend-design Redesign the full marketing website for `client/` as **Injaaz Digital**, an existing Next.js + Tailwind website with bilingual support (`en` and `ar`), and elevate it into a premium, trust-first digital growth brand for ambitious businesses and creators.

This is not a startup template refresh and not a playful concept piece. The redesign should move away from anything that feels generic, gadget-driven, over-animated, or visually noisy. It should feel like a serious modern company that combines strategy, design, systems, and digital execution with calm confidence.

The overall impression should be:
- premium
- trustworthy
- clear
- mature
- editorial-clean
- strategic
- quietly high-performance
- custom-designed, not AI-generic

The site should feel inspired by the quality bar of world-class digital brands and agencies:
- the clarity and conversion discipline of companies like HubSpot
- the polished system thinking and premium restraint associated with Silicon Valley product brands
- the calm confidence, structure, and case-study maturity of high-end agencies

But do **not** copy any one brand literally. Build something original for **Injaaz Digital**.

## Brand Positioning Direction

Injaaz Digital should feel like a modern digital growth partner for businesses that want:
- a more credible online presence
- better conversion and lead flow
- stronger brand perception
- smoother digital systems
- cleaner execution across website, content, funnel, CRM, automation, and growth

The site should communicate that Injaaz Digital does not just “make websites.”
It helps clients create a more serious digital presence, better business flow, and long-term growth infrastructure.

The design must balance:
- business trust
- design sophistication
- operational clarity
- subtle technology fluency
- premium service positioning

## Overall UX / UI Goals

Design goals:
- trust-first premium presentation
- clean professional hierarchy
- restrained sophistication
- subtle motion only where it improves polish
- stronger sense of competence, structure, and business maturity
- clear service communication
- premium spacing and visual rhythm
- elegant bilingual execution for both LTR and RTL

The site should feel:
- less like a SaaS landing page
- less like a freelancer portfolio
- less like a trendy agency trying too hard
- more like a refined strategic digital firm with strong taste and real business value

## Avoid These Directions Entirely

Do not use:
- playful doodles
- cartoonish illustrations
- startup cliches
- loud gradients everywhere
- flashy glassmorphism
- random glowing AI orbs
- crypto / hacker / futuristic gadget vibes
- overuse of floating UI windows with no purpose
- generic centered hero with text stacked over two buttons and a fake dashboard
- visual clutter
- excessive animation
- trendy-for-the-sake-of-trendy typography
- anything that feels like a mass-market template

## Visual Direction

Use a premium visual system with:
- asymmetric or intentionally composed layouts
- editorial spacing
- strong grid discipline
- refined blue / white / neutral palette aligned with the existing brand direction
- possible accents in deep navy, muted slate, soft steel, and very restrained warm neutrals if needed
- rounded but controlled geometry
- crisp surfaces with soft depth
- thin borders, subtle highlights, and premium card layering
- polished section transitions
- elegant typography hierarchy that feels established and credible

The visual language should feel:
- high-end
- calm
- intelligent
- service-oriented
- sharp but not aggressive
- premium without looking expensive-for-show

## Motion Direction

Motion should be visible but quiet:
- slow ambient movement in background layers
- gentle fade/slide reveals
- subtle hover elevation
- delicate border sweeps
- light parallax only if extremely restrained
- polished CTA interactions
- no distracting animation loops
- no noisy pulsing everywhere

It must keep these core elements:
- heading
- subheading
- primary CTA
- secondary CTA

Preferred hero direction:

- message-first trust positioning
- premium typography
- clear CTA hierarchy
- a refined visual centerpiece that communicates business transformation, clarity, and execution

Instead, the visual centerpiece can be something like:

- layered interface snapshots showing brand, website, funnel, CRM, content, and performance in a calm integrated way

The centerpiece should suggest:
- visibility
- structure

It should not feel like:
- a fake SaaS product screenshot
- a random dashboard collage
- a loud 3D illustration
